  • Directions to Enterprise

If we’re driving from the Nest: Exit The Nest turning right on R864 and proceed all the way up to N6 and take a right. Stay on N6 past the N84 interchange. At the next interchange swing left (north) onto N83. Very quickly we will come to another Circle K followed by a car dealer (Monaghans).

Get in right turn lane and turn right up the hill. The white building houses Enterprise Rentals.

Probably Uber out to Enterprise on August 28th and Uber back on September 9. If we drive back to the Nest to pick up bags follow directions at end of self drive tour (day 8).
