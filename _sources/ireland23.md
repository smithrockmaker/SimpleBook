## Ireland 2023!

Dawn signed up for a printmaking workshop in Ireland in August of 2023. Bernie and Ladd who created Pan Pastels have roots in Ireland and have been asking us when we would like to visit. I am finally retired. Moe Carrick has some contacts in the Irish outdoor business community. Put it all together and the universe was begging us to both go and spend some time together in Ireland.

### Book Structure:

As in past books this one is intended to be a repository for the many notes and references that we may want to keep track of as we plan this trip and travel.
